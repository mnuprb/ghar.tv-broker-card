In plain words

What is a Ghar.tv Card?

A free professional profile and digital business card for real estate brokers in India.

Free foreverFor brokers, channel partners and consultantsIndiaAbout 90 seconds to claim

A Ghar.tv Card is built for brokers, channel partners and property consultants. It gives you a personal URL at ghar.tv/your-name, a QR code, a digital visiting card, your listings, reviews, social links and a MahaRERA verified badge if you are a registered agent. It is free to create, publish and share, and it stays free.

Unlike a generic digital business card, a Ghar.tv Card is built specifically for real estate professionals and automatically stays updated with listings, market intelligence and verification.

You pick the localities you work in and post your own properties, which takes seconds and can be done from the moment you claim. Automatic listing sync from WhatsApp is available as an optional upgrade.

Buyers who find your card reach you directly by call or WhatsApp, and enquiries are never sold on or shared.

Every Monday, card holders get one WhatsApp message with what is worth knowing in their markets: new launches, price movement, infrastructure updates and buyer demand trends. A card takes about 90 seconds to claim and can be deleted at any time.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is the card really free?

Yes. Your card, your identity, your listings, your socials and your reputation are free forever. You can build your whole business off it and never pay us a rupee. Optional upgrades exist for brokers who want an edge - GBX Market Exchange, WhatsApp Listing Sync, White Glove Setup and priority visibility - but you never need them to use your card fully.

Do I have to be exclusive to Ghar.tv?

No. Keep working exactly as you do today. The card is yours to use alongside everything else.

Do I need to upload my listings?

You post your own listings, and it takes seconds. From the moment you claim your card you can add, edit and remove properties yourself. If you would rather not do it manually, WhatsApp Listing Sync is an upgrade that adds listings for you from the messages you already send.

Do you guarantee leads?

No, and we would not claim to. What we promise is a professional page that makes you easier to find and trust, plus one useful message a week about your markets. When buyers do enquire, they reach you directly.

Who sees my phone number?

Buyers reach you through the card by call or WhatsApp. Your number is not displayed publicly unless you choose to show it.

What if I am not MahaRERA registered?

You can still create your card. Every card carries an identity verified badge once your number is confirmed. Registered agents carry a RERA verified badge instead, checked against the MahaRERA register. Nothing negative ever shows on your card. The one thing registration unlocks is listing new and under construction projects, which needs a registration number by law. Rentals and resale do not.

Can I link my MagicBricks, 99acres or Housing profile?

Yes, and we would encourage it. Add any portal profile, your Google reviews, your website, your social channels and anything else you use. Ghar.tv does not replace the tools you already work with, it brings them together in one place. There is no lock-in and no exclusivity.

Can I remove my card?

Yes, in one tap, at any time. No questions and no follow-up messages.
